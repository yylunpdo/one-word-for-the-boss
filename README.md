Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty
[One Word for the Boss](https://make.fun/bounty/u-swift-badger-44-one-word-for-the-boss-6b48c735):
send exactly one word that describes your boss — no phrase, no caption,
no explanation. The word is the whole entry.

## The word

**Neckbreather** — the boss who is always standing right behind your
chair, breathing down your neck while you work. Funny on its own, true
of a habit every office worker has met, and fresh next to the first
words anyone reaches for.

## How it works

`main.py` (pure Python standard library, no dependencies) writes that
single word to `/output/entry.txt`:

```
Neckbreather
```

Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
